Privacy

Your minutes stay yours.

Tribe works because your friends can see whether you stayed under your limit. It never needs to see how long you were on your phone, or what you did there. This page says what Tribe keeps, what never leaves your iPhone, and who can see what.

Last updated 12 October 2026. Tribe is still in development, so this describes the app as it is built today, in plain words. A formal privacy policy comes before launch.

What never leaves your iPhone

These stay on your phone. Tribe's servers never receive them, so nobody, including us, can see them.

  • How many minutes you spent on your phone, or in any app
  • Which apps you opened, when, and what you did in them
  • The apps you pick in Apple's Screen Time picker
  • Your age, and the daily average you typed in when you set up
  • Your contacts and your clipboard. Tribe never asks for or reads either

What we keep, and who sees it

This is what Tribe stores on its servers. The only other things are short-lived counters that stop anyone spamming searches or guessing invite codes.

What your tribe sees

Everyone in a tribe agrees to the same limit and the same apps, so they all know what's being counted. Each day they see:

  • Your status, such as on track, under, over, or not heard from yet.
  • When your phone last checked in, when it has been a while.
  • Your username, name and photo, and the tribe's streak, shields and bananas.

They never see your minutes, what time you went over, your time zone, the other apps you use, your bananas, your badges or your outfit. Someone you invite can see your profile before they join.

How long we keep it

Tribe deletes what it no longer needs every night:

  • Notifications we sent: 30 days.
  • Invites: 30 days after they're accepted, run out or are cancelled. An invite someone declined stays as long as the tribe and both accounts do, so nobody can keep inviting a person who said no.
  • The reports that you went over a limit: 60 days. Whether each day was under or over stays, because it's your tribe's history.
  • Wrong invite-code tries: 1 day.
  • Everything else, your account, tribes, day history, bananas and badges: until you delete your account.

Things Tribe doesn't do

  • No selling your data, and no giving it out: not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics companies built into the app.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No reading your contacts, photo library or clipboard. If you add a photo, you pick that one photo.
  • No showing your email address to anyone.

Who else handles it

Tribe runs on a few services. They store or deliver your data so the app works, and that is all we use them for.

  • Supabase hosts Tribe's database, sign-in and photos. Every table is locked so a person can only read what this page says they can.
  • Apple runs Screen Time on your iPhone, Sign in with Apple, and the delivery of notifications.
  • Google handles sign-in, if you choose Sign in with Google.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete Account, then Delete everything. That deletes your account, profile, photo, memberships, day history, goal history, bananas, badges, outfit and monkey name.

  • If you own a tribe that still has other members, delete that tribe first, so it never disappears from under them.
  • A tribe's own record stays with the tribe: its streak and which days it won.
  • Your settings on the phone stay until you delete the app.
  • If you used Sign in with Google, you can also remove Tribe from your Google account's settings.

This website

  • The waitlist keeps only the email address you type and when you joined. It is used to tell you when the beta opens, and nothing else.
  • The site has no analytics or tracking. Its fonts come from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which see your browser's address when the page loads.

Kids, and changes

Tribe isn't made for children. It's meant for people 13 and older. If anything on this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
