Tribe, in your hand.

Friends form a tribe, agree on a daily screen-time limit, and pass or fail the day together. Each member's monkey shows how their day is going.

Try this

  1. Wake your monkey and give it a name.
  2. On Home, tap No-Scroll Squad to see your tribe's day.
  3. Tap + to start a tribe and invite friends.
  4. In the Shop, try a hat on your monkey.
