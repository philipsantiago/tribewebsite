The good stuff

Built to get you off the app.

Your tribe streak grows every day you all make it. You also keep a personal streak that's yours alone.

Good questions.

How does Tribe know my screen time?

Tribe uses Apple's official Screen Time framework. You pick the apps and set a limit, and iOS tells Tribe when you've gone over. Tribe never sees what you did in those apps.

What can my friends actually see?

A ✓ or an ✗ for each day. Never your minutes, never when you went over, and never what you did on your phone. Everything they can and can't see.

What happens if one person goes over?

The tribe's day fails and the streak goes back to zero for everyone. Nobody loses bananas they already earned. If your tribe has a Shield, it saves the streak automatically.

How big can a tribe be?

2 to 6 people. Small enough that everyone matters, big enough to be a party. You can be in more than one tribe, each with its own limit.

What if my phone dies all day?

A dead phone doesn't fail your tribe. You won't earn bananas that day, and your personal streak just pauses. It doesn't break.

Is it free?

Yes. And we'll never sell streak repairs or shields.
